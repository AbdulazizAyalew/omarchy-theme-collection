# Wallpaper photography and licensing

The installable wallpaper derivatives were cropped to 16:10, resized to
2880×1800, and given a subtle dark/cool color grade. Full-resolution source
files are omitted from this collection to keep downloads smaller; use the
linked source pages to obtain them.
